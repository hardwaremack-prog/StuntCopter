# StuntCopter (browser recreation)

A fan recreation of **StuntCopter**, the 1986 Macintosh shareware game by Duane Blehm, playable in any web browser. It's one self-contained HTML file with no downloads or installs, styled like a vintage compact Mac.

All graphics and sounds here are drawn and synthesized in code. No original game files, artwork or code are included. StuntCopter and its original design belong to Duane Blehm and his estate.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari (desktop or phone).

- **Move** the mouse (or drag a finger) to fly the helicopter
- **Click / tap / Space** to drop the stuntman
- Land him in the **hay**. Hitting the horse or the driver costs 2 jumpers; missing costs 1
- The higher you drop from, the more points you get
- Clouds slow his fall
- Land 5 in a row to level up: the wagon gets faster and the hay gets smaller
- **Level 5+:** killer birds go after the copter. **Right-click**, **F**, **Ctrl** or the on-screen **FIRE** button to shoot them. You get 3 copters
- Arrow keys also fly the copter

High scores (3 initials) are saved in your browser.
